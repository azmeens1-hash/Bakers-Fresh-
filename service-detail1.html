Wedding Cakes

Wedding Cakes — A Centrepiece as Unforgettable as Your Day

5–14 Days Lead Time
Multi-Tier Masterpieces
White Glove Delivery
Free Design Consultation

Your wedding cake is more than dessert — it is the living centrepiece of your most important day, photographed from every angle, admired by every guest, and remembered for years to come. At Baker's Fresh, we approach every wedding cake as a bespoke work of edible art, crafted from scratch to reflect your story, your style, and your celebration.

From intimate two-tier cakes draped in hand-painted watercolour florals, to towering five-tier showstoppers adorned with sugar flowers and real gold leaf — our team of dedicated cake artists will translate your vision into something genuinely breathtaking. No template. No shortcuts. Every cake is designed exclusively for you.

We use only premium, fresh ingredients — imported Belgian chocolate, real Chantilly cream, farm-sourced eggs and natural food colours — ensuring every slice tastes as spectacular as the cake looks. Because on your wedding day, nothing should be anything less than perfect.

What's Included

Complimentary 1-hour design consultation with our lead cake artist

Multiple tier options — 2, 3, 4 and 5+ tier configurations available

Buttercream, fondant or semi-naked finish — your choice
Fresh or sugar-crafted floral decorations, gold leaf and custom toppers

Popular Wedding Cake Styles

Minimalist Marble & Stone — slate grey fondant with gold leaf accents
Pressed Flower Cascade — edible fresh or dried florals draped across tiers
Naked & Semi-Naked — rustic sponge layers with minimal frosting
Royal Luxury — full fondant with hand-painted motifs and metallic lustre
Floral Garden — cascading sugar flowers in your wedding colour palette
Modern Geometric — architectural panels with sharp lines and metallic accents

Our Wedding Cake Process

01 · Enquire Share your wedding date, guest count and initial style ideas with us.
02 · Consult We meet (in person or virtually) for a 1-hour design and tasting session.
03 · Design Our artist creates a bespoke design sketch for your approval.
04 · Craft Your cake is handcrafted over 3–5 days using fresh premium ingredients.
05 · Deliver White-glove delivery, assembly and setup at your venue on the day.
