✦ Our Story

Baked with Passion,
Served with Pride

Baker's Fresh was born in 2015 from a tiny kitchen in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad. Founder Lakshmi Reddy believed every celebration deserves a cake that's as special as the moment itself — not just delicious, but visually breathtaking.

What started as weekend orders for friends and family quickly grew into Hyderabad's most-loved artisan bakery. Today, we serve over 500 families, crafting everything from intimate anniversary cakes to grand multi-tiered wedding showstoppers.

Our philosophy: where visual inspiration meets logistical precision. We don't just bake — we create experiences that last far longer than the last crumb.
